My Journey

Hello there! My name is Raihansyah but you can call me Dean. I am a passionate game producer, designer, and technical artist from Indonesia! Currently I am pursuing my Master's in Computer Science of game development at University of Southern California (USC). My journey in game development has taken me from building video game RPGs, developing board games, and to crafting unique puzzle mechanics but always with a focus on delivering memorable player experiences. My goal is to create engaging and innovative experiences.
