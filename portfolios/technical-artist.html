Raihansyah A. Andrian

Technical Artist

Hello there! My name is Raihansyah but you can call me Dean. I am a passionate technical artist from Indonesia! Currently I am pursuing my Master's in Computer Science of game development at University of Southern California (USC). Technical art is a field that I feel a lot of people don't fully understand, yet you can do so many things in it such as modeling, rigging, animation, and VFX. Having a strong background in mathematics and computer science, I have always loved how we can use them to create extraordinary things. This field is a direct application of it since I can clearly show what I am creating and be proud of it. I have first-hand experience in applying several methods of rendering including applying it in game engines. I also have knowledge of how to create animation using both a 3D software such as Maya or computer generated based as well. These include basic methods of motion capture interpolation, skinning, and inverse-kinematics (IK). My goal as a developer is to create engaging and innovative experiences.
