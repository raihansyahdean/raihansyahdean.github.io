Raihansyah A. Andrian

Game Designer

Hello there! My name is Raihansyah but you can call me Dean. I am a passionate game designer from Indonesia! Currently I am pursuing my Master's in Computer Science of game development at University of Southern California (USC). I am particularly interested in game design because there are unlimited potential on how we can create games and what we create are the "Aha!" and jaw-dropping moments that players get excited about. I start design development by staking out the foundation of the narrative world-building and experience goal of the game. After a base is set in stone, I would collaboratively iterate on the particular systems that would support that foundation through prototyping and balancing. As a designer, I value being able to document my design decisions well so that the game can be implemented and visualized as intended. My goal as a developer is to create engaging and innovative experiences.
